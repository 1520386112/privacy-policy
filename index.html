App Activity

We may collect information about how users interact with the App, including:

  • Game sessions
  • Level starts and completions

  • Feature usage
  • In-game actions and interactions
  • Session duration

  • General gameplay behavior

This information is used for analytics, game balancing, product improvement, and understanding how players interact with the App.

Device or Other Identifiers

The App and third-party services may collect identifiers associated with a device, installation, or application instance, including:

  • App installation identifiers

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To detect and fix crashes, errors, and technical problems

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may independently collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

GameAnalytics

We use GameAnalytics to understand gameplay activity, game performance, player interaction, and usage patterns.

GameAnalytics may process gameplay events, session information, device identifiers, technical information, and approximate geographic information inferred from IP addresses.

More information: https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-notice

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to identify crashes, diagnose errors, and improve the stability of the App.

Firebase Crashlytics may process information including crash stack traces, application state, device metadata, operating system information, installation identifiers, and diagnostic information.

More information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

AppLovin

We use AppLovin and AppLovin MAX to provide, manage, and measure advertising within the App.

AppLovin and advertising partners available through AppLovin MAX may collect or process information such as device information, application information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, network information, ad interaction data, and advertising performance information.

This information may be used for:

  • Serving advertisements
  • Measuring ad performance
  • Advertising attribution
  • Fraud prevention
  • Frequency capping
  • Improving advertising services
  • Personalized advertising where permitted and where user consent has been obtained

AppLovin MAX may also mediate advertisements from third-party advertising networks. Those advertising partners may process information according to their own privacy policies.

More information: https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/
